## 📜 Mayor missions

A short chain of objectives that pay money and XP: roads, a cottage, rent, population, a cafe, a park, mayor level, and a map expansion.

## 🏙️ A clearer city

Gradient sky, three-tone grass, and a bit more detail on cottages, villas, and the tech office.

## ⏱️ Build time follows cost

Expensive buildings take longer, and decorations are no longer almost instant. Saves no longer send the whole city back.
