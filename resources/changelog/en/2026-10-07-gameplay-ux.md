## 🖱️ Smarter Cursors & Tile Highlights

The canvas cursor reflects build, demolish, collect, and inspect modes with hover highlights on tiles.

## 💰 Demolition Refunds

Tearing down a structure returns 50% of its original build cost to your treasury.

## 🗺️ Level-Based Map Growth

Expand your city grid every 5 mayor levels starting at level 5 — no treasury cap.

## 💾 Action-Based Auto-Save

Progress saves when you build, finish construction, demolish, or collect — one save when collecting all.
